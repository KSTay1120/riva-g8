# The Riva Residences App

## How to Deploy to Vercel (Step by Step)

### Step 1 — Go to vercel.com
Open this link on your computer: https://vercel.com

### Step 2 — Sign up
Click "Sign Up" → choose "Continue with GitHub"
If you don't have GitHub: go to github.com → sign up free first

### Step 3 — Deploy
1. Click "Add New..." → "Project"
2. Click "Browse" or drag this whole folder into the upload area
3. Vercel auto-detects it as a React app
4. Click "Deploy" (big blue button)
5. Wait ~2 minutes

### Step 4 — Get your link
You'll get a link like: https://riva-residences-xxx.vercel.app
Share this link with clients!

### Step 5 — Clients install it
Tell clients: "Open the link → tap Share → Add to Home Screen"
It will look exactly like an app on their phone!
